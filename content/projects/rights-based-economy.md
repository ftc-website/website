This project monitors global economic policy and governance debates from a rights-based economy perspective, to build a basis for advocating for alternative economic and financial policies that are not driven by profit, wealth, growth or extractivism, but by a human rights-aligned vision of the economy.

The FTC builds a narrative of an economic system anchored in a feminist, decolonial, anti-racist and anti-fascist political economy: a Rights-Based Economy, which is both a lens for analysing the human rights aspects of alternative proposals and an alternative in itself.
