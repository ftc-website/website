The People's Fiscal Monitor tracks how financial transparency enables taxing wealth, high incomes, large corporations and extractive industries to finance care systems and social protection in the Global South, connecting with like-minded groups in the Global North to align monitoring and evidence building.

It builds on the coalition's work with the End Austerity Campaign, co-created during the COVID-19 pandemic to analyse recovery spending in Global South countries, the austerity policies of international financial institutions, and alternatives such as taxing wealth and large companies.
