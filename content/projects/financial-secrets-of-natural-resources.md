This project monitors natural resources, forced labour and illicit financial flows related to extractive industries in fisheries, forestry, mining and commercial agriculture, and builds the case for public disclosure of the beneficial ownership of companies and assets linked to natural resources.

Since 2021 the FTC has worked with fisheries sector organisations and trade unions to uncover the companies and beneficial owners behind illegal, unreported and unregulated fishing and forced labour on fishing vessels, through joint investigations, training and collective demands.

The report *Financial Secrets of the Forests* extends this work to the forestry sector.
