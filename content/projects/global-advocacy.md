---
title: Global advocacy
summary: Where we bring the Global Charter on Financial Transparency in international processes.
order: 4
---

- **UN Tax Convention**, to achieve the ambition of the ABC-DEFG of financial transparency, together with regional spaces where public disclosure is discussed (African Union, European Union, the Latin American and Caribbean Tax Platform).
- **IMF and World Bank**, to hold them accountable for austerity policies, and to make the public case for progressive taxation to fund public services.
- **UNODC and UNCTAD** on illicit financial flows (SDG 16.4.1), so that environmental crimes and human rights violations are part of anti-IFF tracking, and tax-related flows are effectively measured.
- **UN Financing for Development**, so that public, and not only administrative, financial transparency becomes the standard after Sevilla.
- **Human rights treaty bodies**, to promote public transparency and feed financial transparency issues into their monitoring.
