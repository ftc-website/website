## How we are governed

The FTC is a membership coalition. All members appoint a representative to the **Coordination Committee**, and two members at a time serve on the **Executive Committee**, which oversees the operations of the International Secretariat.

Since 2025 the FTC has been building a board of global scientific advisors drawn from academia and experienced professionals, who provide input into each project and into the Secretariat's operations.

## Working groups

- ABC-DEFG of Financial Transparency: tracking financial secrecy and its enablers
- People's Fiscal Monitor: tracking progressive taxation to fund care and social protection
- Illicit financial flows in fisheries, forestry and critical minerals
- Rebuilding multilateralism towards a rights-based economy

## International Secretariat

The Secretariat is hosted by LATINDADD in Peru and led by the Executive Director, Matti Kohonen.
