The Financial Transparency Coalition (FTC) believes in transparency, including about how we use your data. We collect as little personal data as possible, we never sell it, and we do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

## Who is responsible

The data controller is the Financial Transparency Coalition, International Secretariat, c/o LATINDADD, Jr Mariscal Miller 2622, Lince, Lima 15073, Peru. For any question about your data, write to [info@financialtransparency.org](mailto:info@financialtransparency.org).

## Visiting this website

**No cookies.** This website does not set cookies or store anything on your device to follow you.

**Visit statistics.** We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which does not use cookies, does not fingerprint visitors and does not store IP addresses. It records the page visited, the referring site, the type of browser and device, the country and page loading times, only in aggregate. We use these figures to understand which content is useful and to improve the site.

**Server logs.** Like any website, our hosting provider ([hosting provider, e.g. Hostinger]) temporarily records technical data such as IP addresses to keep the site secure and working. These logs are deleted automatically after [retention period].

**Fonts.** The typefaces are served from our own website, not from third parties.

## Newsletter

If you subscribe to our newsletter, we collect your email address and, if you give them, your name, organisation and preferred language. We use them only to send you FTC news, publications and event invitations.

- **Legal basis:** your consent, which you give by subscribing and confirming your subscription by email.
- **Provider:** our mailing list is managed by Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, France), which stores data in the European Union.
- **Statistics:** our emails may record whether they were opened and which links were clicked, so we can see what readers find useful.
- **Unsubscribing:** every email has an unsubscribe link. You can also write to us and we will remove you.
- **Retention:** we keep your details while you are subscribed and delete them after you unsubscribe.

## Contacting us

When you write to us by email, we use your message and contact details only to reply, and we keep them as long as needed to handle your request.

## Sharing your data

We do not sell, rent or share your personal data with anyone, except the service providers named above, which process it only on our behalf.

## Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to its use, or to withdraw your consent at any time, by writing to [info@financialtransparency.org](mailto:info@financialtransparency.org). We will reply within [30] days. You also have the right to complain to your data protection authority; in Peru, the Autoridad Nacional de Protección de Datos Personales.

## Links to other websites

Our pages link to reports and websites of members and partners. Their own privacy policies apply when you visit them.

## Changes

We will publish any change to this policy on this page, with its date.
