## Our vision

A world where we know who owns what, where and how, and whether they meet their tax obligations and are taxed progressively according to their capabilities, so that transparency helps distribute power, income and wealth, and holds accountable those responsible for human rights and environmental violations.

## Our mission

To demonstrate, harness and advocate for the power of financial transparency in order to build a rights-based, environmentally balanced and fairer global society and economy.

## Our approach

We cannot promote change alone. System change requires a cross-movement approach, in which the FTC and its members bring the vision of financial transparency to the issues other movements are tackling, and apply the lens of financial transparency to their work, and vice versa. We do this in three ways:

- **Producing knowledge** that documents and exposes tax abuses and illicit financial flows, and how secrecy undermines human rights and enables environmental crimes.
- **Sharing knowledge**, building the capacity of others, and elevating knowledge from other networks, coalitions, campaigns and social movements.
- **Engaging in advocacy** in global spaces, and enabling the advocacy of our members and allies in regional and national spaces.

## Our history

Founded in 2009 as the Task Force on Financial Integrity and Economic Development, the coalition helped ensure that illicit financial flows were included in the 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development. In 2013 it became a civil society coalition, defining its goals around the ABC of Financial Transparency. Between 2013 and 2017 it expanded into a Global South-led network, with members in Latin America and the Caribbean, Africa, Asia and Europe.

With the 2026–2030 strategy, the ABC has become the ABC-DEFG of Financial Transparency: a [Global Charter on Financial Transparency](/our-work/global-charter/).
