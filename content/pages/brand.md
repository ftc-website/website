Members, partners and journalists can use the FTC logo to refer to the coalition and its work. Please follow the Brand Guidelines and do not alter the logo.

## Downloads

- **[Brand Guidelines (PDF, 13 pages)](/files/brand/FTC-Brand-Guidelines.pdf)**: how to use the logo, colours, typefaces and graphic elements.
- **[Complete brand kit (ZIP)](/files/brand/FTC-brand-kit.zip)**: every logo version in SVG and PNG, the colour palette, social media images and the email signature.

## Colours and typefaces

Navy `#263866` and FTC Blue `#3D86AE` are the main colours, with Amber `#F2B233` as an accent. Headings use Archivo and running text uses Inter; both are free from Google Fonts.

## Questions

For media enquiries or permission to use the logo in a co-branded piece, write to [info@financialtransparency.org](mailto:info@financialtransparency.org).
