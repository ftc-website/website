The strategy presents an updated set of policy asks, the ABC-DEFG of Financial Transparency, which we also call the [Global Charter on Financial Transparency](/our-work/global-charter/). We call on all States, regional and international bodies to adopt it, and on civil society, academia and other stakeholders to promote it.

> Public access is not an add-on. Without public scrutiny, there will be no end to illicit financial flows.

## Three intertwined crises

In this strategic period we focus on three crises:

- **The crisis of care** and the lack of social security, which should be funded by taxing wealth, corporations, extractive industries and high incomes.
- **The crisis of illicit fisheries, forestry and critical mineral mining**, which threatens ecosystems, harms efforts to tackle climate change and causes human rights violations.
- **The crisis of multilateralism**, where oligarchic and ultra-wealthy interests erode international cooperation and democracy.

## Our overarching goal

To analyse financial transparency and expose financial secrecy, making the findings accessible to everyone; and to mobilise a cross-movement strategy that uses this data and knowledge to build people's power and challenge austerity, concentration of power, environmental crises and human rights violations.
