## Coordination Committee

Every member appoints a representative to the Coordination Committee. The members that guided the 2026–2030 strategy are:

- Asian Peoples' Movement on Debt and Development (APMDD)
- CEFILAT
- Christian Aid
- Eurodad
- Global Financial Integrity (GFI)
- LATINDADD
- Pan African Lawyers Union (PALU)
- Tax Justice Network
- Tax Justice Network Africa
- Transparency International

## Allied organisations

During this strategic period the FTC is opening up to new members and allied organisations. An organisation can apply to become an allied organisation and, once approved by the Coordination Committee, take part in the working groups the FTC coordinates globally. Allies can separately apply to join the Coordination Committee, which should be regionally and thematically balanced.
