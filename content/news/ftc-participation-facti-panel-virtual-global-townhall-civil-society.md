The High-Level Panel on International Financial Accountability, Transparency and Integrity for Achieving the 2030 Agenda (FACTI Panel) is holding a series of consultations with different stakeholders, during the of April and May.

As part of these consultations, a virtual global townhall with civil society took place last Thursday, April 28, in which the Financial Transparency Coalition FTC, as well as many of its members, were able to participate.
