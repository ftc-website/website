We are sad to announce the permanent cancellation of the FTC Conference “*From Boiling Point to Turning Point*”, having postponed the planned event in Bangkok earlier this month.

The unprecedented global context given the COVID-19 outbreak have led us to this decision.

The FTC is now prioritizing collaboration with allies and partners to contribute to mitigating the social and economic impacts of this crisis, especially in the Global South.

Do please continue to monitor our website as we will be announcing new virtual events and initiatives shortly.

Thank you for your interest in and support of the FTC.
