Today, April 24, the High-level Panel on Financial Accountability, Transparency & Integrity (FACTI) is holding its [first virtual consultation with United Nations Member States](https://www.factipanel.org/events/virtual-consultation-with-united-nations-member-states)

The consultation aims to hear from member States their views as the priority actions for promoting financial accountability, transparency and integrity for achieving the 2030 Agenda.

A letter was submitted by the FTC to the representatives of the G77 countries to make some policy recommendations on the occasion of this consultation. The submission includes the Financial Transparency Coalition´s views as to the critical points that the FACTI panel should consider to tackle the challenges the world is facing in relation to financial accountability, transparency and integrity.
