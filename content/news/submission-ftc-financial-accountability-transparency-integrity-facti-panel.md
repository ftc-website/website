The submission was prepared by the FTC's Independent Working Group on Illicit Financial Flows.

A letter was submitted by the FTC to the Financial Accountability, Transparency and Integrity Panel (FACTI Panel) to make recommendations on aligning the purpose of the FACTI Panel with human rights and the Sustainable Development Goals.  Specific feedback was also provided on the questions for consultation.

Please click here to read the [FTC's Submission to the FACTI Panel](/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/FTCs-Submission-to-FACTI-Panel-1.pdf).
