In September 2015, ChristianAid and the FTC launched 'SwissLeaks Reviewed', a project that analyzed the data from SwissLeaks, but instead interpreted the dollar amounts attributed to countries as a percentage of GDP, rather than looking at them in absolute terms. You can view the full site at [swissleaksreviewed.org](http://www.swissleaksreviewed.org).
