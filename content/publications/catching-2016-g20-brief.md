This briefing, released ahead of the 2016 G20 Summit in Hangzhou, China outlines some of the latest commitments to transparency made by G20 countries that actually go further than commitments they've made together. For the G20 to be a serious leader on issues of financial transparency, it's vital that they move forward with bold transparency reforms, especially in the light of recent scandals, including the Panama Papers.
