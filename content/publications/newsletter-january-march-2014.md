After a hiatus, January 2014 saw the reinstitution of the FTC's quarterly newsletter, bringing our networks updates on policy, media, and the organization. Some of the highlights from the March 2014 newsletter include the addition of three new Coordinating Committee members to the FTC, a vote by the European Parliament showing support for public registers of beneficial ownership, and updates on our work in the media.
