#### **Submission Date: June 15th, 2020**

The Independent Working Group on Illicit Financial Flows, a project of the Financial Transparency Coalition (FTC), coordinated a submission to the UN Tax Committee ahead of the 20th Session of the Committee of Experts on International Cooperation on Tax Matters (22-26th June 2020) to share recommendations and feedback.
