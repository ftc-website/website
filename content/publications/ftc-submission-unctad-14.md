Ahead of the 14th Session of the United Nations Conference on Trade and Development, the FTC has submitted recommendations on why illicit financial flows should be a central pillar of the conference, and the broader agenda of trade and development. UNCTAD 14 [will take place](http://unctad14.org/en/Pages/Home.aspx) in Nairobi, Kenya from 17-22 July 2016.
