Prior to their meeting in Washington, D.C. on April 10-11, 2014, the Financial Transparency Coalition submitted a letter to each Minister outlining the need for the G20 to act on three issues central to illicit financial flows: collection of beneficial ownership information, public country by country reporting for multi-national companies, and the automatic exchange of financial information between nations.
