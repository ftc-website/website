The global financial and economic system faces a crisis of austerity and rising inequality of wealth and income, coupled with an ecological breakdown driven by the illicit overexploitation of fisheries, forestry, minerals and land.

This strategy presents an updated set of policy asks, the ABC-DEFG of Financial Transparency, and three projects for 2026–2030: the People's Fiscal Monitor, Financial Secrets of Natural Resources, and a Human Rights-Based Global Economy.
