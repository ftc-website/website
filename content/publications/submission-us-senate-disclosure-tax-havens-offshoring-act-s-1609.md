#### Submission Date: July 28, 2020.

The Financial Transparency Coalition (FTC), as part of a coalition of more than 100 nonprofit groups representing progressive, labor, civil rights, anti-corruption, and environmental interests submitted a letter to the United States Senate, urging to advance the Disclosure of Tax Havens and Offshoring Act (S. 1609), which would require large, publicly-traded corporations to disclose key financial information on a country-by-country basis to better inform taxpayers, investors, policymakers, academics, and other stakeholders.
