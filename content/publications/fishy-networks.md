Africa concentrates 48.9% of identified industrial and semi-industrial vessels involved in illegal, unreported and unregulated (IUU) fishing, 40% in West Africa alone, which has become a global epicentre for these activities.

The report, the most extensive analysis of IUU fishing cases to date, warns that developing countries lose billions of dollars in illicit money flows directly linked to this practice every year: up to $11.49 billion for Africa, $2 billion for Argentina and $4 billion for Indonesia.

It also reveals that the top 10 companies involved in IUU fishing concentrate nearly one quarter of all reported vessels: eight from China, one from Colombia and another from Spain.
