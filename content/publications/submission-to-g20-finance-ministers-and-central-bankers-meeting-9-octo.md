The FTC has submitted recommendations to G20 Finance Ministers and Central Bankers ahead of their final meeting prior to the 2015 G20 Summit in Turkey. The Ministers will meet on 8 October in Lima, Peru to give their final approval of OECD-led initiatives on the automatic exchange of financial information to curtail tax evasion, and the Base Erosion and Profit Shifting outcomes, a plan to tackle corporate tax abuses.
