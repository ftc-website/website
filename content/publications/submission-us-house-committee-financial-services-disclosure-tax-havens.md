#### Submission Date: July 14th, 2020

The Financial Transparency Coalition (FTC), as part of a coalition of more than 100 nonprofit groups representing progressive, labor, civil rights, anti-corruption, and environmental interests submitted a letter in a US House Committee on Financial Services [hearing](https://financialservices.house.gov/calendar/eventsingle.aspx?EventID=406733) held on Wednesday, 14th of July, calling on Reps. Waters and McHenry to consider country-by-country reporting the Disclosure of Tax Havens and Offshoring Act ([H.R. 5933](https://www.congress.gov/bill/116th-congress/house-bill/5933?s=1&r=4))
