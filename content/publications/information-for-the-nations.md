As the Organization for Economic Cooperation and Development (OECD) and the G20 develop a new standard for cross border information exchange, it's vital that any new system is inclusive. A new briefing from ChristianAid looks at how developing countries are being excluded from the new standards, and how this can be changed. You can also [view our infographic on the subject](/wp-content/uploads/2015/03/AIEinfographic.png) of developing country inclusion.
