*Submission Date: 15 January 2018*

The FTC entered a submission to the OECD's consultation on rules limiting taxpayers' ability to hide their income and assets offshore.  However, as the OECD's consultation itself [noted](http://www.oecd.org/tax/oecd-seeks-input-on-new-tax-rules-requiring-disclosure-of-crs-avoidance-arrangements-and-offshore-structures.htm), many advisers and service providers have been "actively marketing schemes" designed to circumvent the Common Reporting Standard requirements.
