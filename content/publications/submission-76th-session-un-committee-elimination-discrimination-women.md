#### Submission date: June 2020.

The Tax Justice Network, as part of the International Institutional Architecture Working Group of the Financial Transparency Coalition (FTC), submitted a shadow report on the Republic of Panama pertaining to its 8th Periodic Report to the UN Committee on the Elimination of Discrimination against Women (CEDAW), for consideration during the 76th session.

The 76th Session of the CEDAW Committee is due to be rescheduled soon as it has been postponed due to the Coronavirus pandemic.
